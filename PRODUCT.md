# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SillyTavern users who maintain multiple generation presets, versions, and prompt configurations. They use the extension inside an already information-dense application and need to understand save state, find prior versions, group related presets, and recover mistakes without leaving their current workflow.

## Product Purpose

Preset Auto Save is a trustworthy preset-history workspace for SillyTavern. It automatically preserves meaningful preset changes, makes version families easier to navigate, and provides understandable recovery and comparison tools. Success means users can confirm the current state within seconds and can organize or recover presets without worrying that the extension will corrupt or obscure their original data.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, restrained, and clear. The interface should feel calm and competent, use familiar task-oriented interactions, and explain consequential actions without becoming verbose or theatrical.

## Anti-references

- A feature showcase that gives every action equal visual weight.
- Dense nested cards, decorative badges, or persistent controls that compete with the preset records.
- Host-theme accent colors flooding the entire workspace and weakening semantic states.
- Modal dialogs for small reversible edits that can be completed inline.
- Color-only status, hidden keyboard focus, hover-only actions, or icon buttons without readable names.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current state and most likely next action first.
2. Prefer familiar, reversible inline interactions over interruptive dialogs.
3. Preserve stable data identity beneath every presentation or organization feature.
4. Keep secondary tools available without letting them dominate the primary workflow.
5. Make every important state understandable without relying on color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls and relationships, visible keyboard focus, accessible names, and keyboard navigation. Primary touch targets must be at least 44px on compact/mobile layouts. Motion must communicate state and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Text and controls must retain usable contrast in light and dark host themes, and hidden panels must not remain focusable.
